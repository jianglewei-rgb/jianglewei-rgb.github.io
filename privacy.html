Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

MimiLab Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API.

Data the app accesses

With the owner's authorization via Google OAuth, the app reads and writes data of the owner's own YouTube channels: channel details, videos, playlists, thumbnails and comments.

How data is used and stored

Data is used solely to upload, schedule and manage the owner's videos. OAuth tokens and operational records are stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.
